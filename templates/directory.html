Regret (Casual) / Remorse (Formal)

Emotion

Context: "Regret" is used in everyday conversation, while "Remorse" expresses profound moral guilt.

"He felt deep remorse for his thoughtless actions."

🇮🇳 हिंदी: Regret - पछतावा | Remorse - गहरा प्रायश्चित / पछतावा

🚩 मराठी: Regret - पश्चात्ताप | Remorse - तीव्र खंत

Tell (Casual) / Inform (Formal)

Business

Context: "Tell" suits casual conversations, while "Inform" or "Notify" is preferred in corporate emails.

"Please inform the project manager once the review is complete."

🇮🇳 हिंदी: Tell - बताना | Inform - सूचित करना

🚩 मराठी: Tell - सांगणे | Inform - माहिती देणे / सूचित करणे

Want (Casual) / Desire (Formal)

Emotion

Context: "Want" indicates a direct need, whereas "Desire" or "Aspire" conveys deeper ambition.

"She had a strong desire to excel in international debates."

🇮🇳 हिंदी: Want - चाहना | Desire - तीव्र इच्छा / आकांक्षा

🚩 मराठी: Want - इच्छा असणे | Desire - तीव्र आकांक्षा

Look Into (Casual) / Investigate (Formal)

Business

Context: "Look into" is spoken casually, while "Investigate" or "Analyze" is standard in technical reports.

"The audit committee will investigate the discrepancy."

🇮🇳 हिंदी: Look into - देखना | Investigate - गहन जांच करना

🚩 मराठी: Look into - पाहणे | Investigate - सखोल तपासणी करणे

Clear (Casual) / Articulate (Formal)

Academic

Context: "Clear" describes understandable words, while "Articulate" denotes persuasive, refined speech.

"He is an articulate speaker who captivated the entire auditorium."

🇮🇳 हिंदी: Clear - साफ | Articulate - स्पष्ट एवं प्रभावशाली वक्ता

🚩 मराठी: Clear - स्पष्ट | Articulate - प्रभावी व स्पष्ट बोलणारा

Help (Casual) / Facilitate (Formal)

Daily Spoken

Context: "Help" is ubiquitous, while "Facilitate" is used when making a process or transition easier.

"Modern software facilitates remote team collaboration."

🇮🇳 हिंदी: Help - मदद | Facilitate - सुगम / सरल बनाना

🚩 मराठी: Help - मदत | Facilitate - सुलभ करणे

Careful (Casual) / Meticulous (Formal)

Academic

Context: "Meticulous" denotes extreme, thorough attention to precision and detail.

"The researcher was meticulous in documenting every laboratory variable."

🇮🇳 हिंदी: Careful - सावधान | Meticulous - अत्यंत सूक्ष्म एवं सटीक

🚩 मराठी: Careful - काळजीपूर्वक | Meticulous - अतिशय बारकाईने काम करणारा

Get (Casual) / Acquire (Formal)

Business

Context: "Get" is casual; "Acquire" or "Obtain" is used for business assets, skills, or knowledge.

"TalkPro helps students acquire natural English speaking abilities."

🇮🇳 हिंदी: Get - पाना | Acquire - अर्जित / प्राप्त करना

🚩 मराठी: Get - मिळवणे | Acquire - आत्मसात करणे / संपादन करणे
